TF2 Frag Demo Helper - Manual HLAE Camera Build
Source build ID: manual-hlae-camera-v1
Prepared: 2026-08-28 UTC

This archive removes the experimental automatic camera planner and replaces its
selector with a single-candidate manual HLAE launch workflow.

Manual HLAE camera behavior:
- Select exactly one candidate and click Launch TF2 with HLAE.
- Uses the existing Before first tick selector as the automatic seek point,
  reaches it from tick 0 using jumps of no more than 15,000 ticks, pauses there,
  and uses the After last tick selector as the end-of-clip reference.
- Key 3 safely reloads the staged demo from tick 0 and repeats the capped forward
  seek instead of making one large backward demo_gototick jump, then reapplies
  third-person/no-viewmodel state before pausing at the lead-in.
- [ advances demo time by 0.25 seconds for precise keyframe placement.
- ] toggles the entire HUD off or on; arrow keys remain free for MIRV camera control.
- 4 cycles through every distinct kill tick for multi-tick candidates.
- 6 establishes third-person/no-viewmodel state and enables mirv_input camera.
  7 adds a campath keyframe. 8 ends manual input, requests third-person/no-viewmodel
  state, and enables campath playback; the guide retains the tested explicit
  thirdperson console check after 8.
- 9/0 start and stop capture using the selected output format, FPS, and encoder.
- The documented recording order is 5 to resume playback, 9 to start, then 0 to stop.
- = saves camera_path.xml beside the manual capture.
- The automatic cinematic selector, planner, victim tracking, and camera-change
  controls are removed. Automatic HLAE recording continues with original camera.
- Manual sessions use the same complete backup/recovery mechanism as automatic
  sessions. Closing TF2 restores cfg/overrides, binds, custom files, HUD,
  hitsounds, config/video files, and DX settings.

Recording readiness behavior:
- The top header reports the active candidate and batch count, such as 20 / 50,
  plus recovery, finalization, consolidation, log archival, and restoration.
- Record with HLAE is disabled while any recording or recovery work is active.
- The button is re-enabled only after the terminal Finished event, or immediately
  if HLAE launch fails before a background recording begins.
- Candidate selections and the recording index are refreshed after every clip
  and after the batch finishes, so another batch can be recorded without
  reloading the exported candidates.
- When TF2 closes early, HLAE is allowed to flush before every usable completed
  capture is finalized, indexed, and reflected on the Candidates page.

Airshot behavior:
- Absolute height is ignored; higher ledges do not qualify as airshots.
- The victim must have an observed grounded-to-airborne transition within two
  seconds, at least three airborne samples, and meaningful vertical motion.
- Standard projectile evidence stops two ticks before impact.
- Loose Cannon evidence stops six ticks before the first collision/hurt event,
  preventing the Cannon's own knockback from certifying a double donk or later
  explosion as an airshot.
- Five regression tests cover stationary and moving elevated ledges, genuine
  pre-impact airborne motion, collision-induced Cannon launch, and genuine
  pre-existing airborne Cannon victims.
- Confirmed airshots receive an additional 0-20 style points based on both the
  victim's positive height relative to the attacker and the upward elevation
  angle from attacker to victim; absolute map Z never earns points.
- New searchable tags identify high-relative, steep-skyward, and extreme
  skyward airshots.
- Two geometry regression tests verify that a shared high ledge earns no bonus
  and that steeper, higher airshots score progressively more.

Bookmark behavior:
- TF2 Demo Support's same-name JSON sidecar is read using its real `events`
  schema, including `ds_mark` bookmark ticks and comments.
- Empty parser-generated bookmark output no longer masks the TF2 sidecar.
- Embedded and sidecar bookmarks are merged and deduplicated.
- Every bookmark creates a qualifying candidate worth 30 points; a bookmark
  near a frag inherits that frag's applicable tags, score, and event data.
- Regression tests cover sidecar parsing, empty-parser fallback, standalone
  bookmark qualification, and nearby-frag inheritance.

Preserved behavior:
- Recording Settings autosave, including initial default settings persistence.
- Saved FFmpeg, HLAE, TF2, and output-directory paths.
- Startup recording-session recovery hardening.
- Vote, server, plugin, and chat-message suppression during capture.
- Previously recorded candidates can be skipped or explicitly re-recorded.

Validation performed in this environment:
- Automatic-camera removal, manual hotkey, candidate seek, multi-kill tick-cycle,
  HLAE launch, and recording-profile restoration cross-reference checks were run.
- Archive source assertions and ZIP integrity are checked during packaging.

The Rust toolchain is not installed in this transient environment. Run
BUILD_RUST_APP.bat on Windows for the complete Rust/Slint compilation.

TF2 Frag Demo Helper - Relative-Height Airshot Scoring Build
Source build ID: 74178911a18e
Prepared: 2026-08-28 UTC

This archive removes the experimental per-kill camera system from the UI,
candidate model, analyzer export, recovery manifest, preview, and recording VDM.

Recording readiness behavior:
- The top header reports the active candidate and batch count, such as 20 / 50,
  plus recovery, finalization, consolidation, log archival, and restoration.
- Record with HLAE is disabled while any recording or recovery work is active.
- The button is re-enabled only after the terminal Finished event, or immediately
  if HLAE launch fails before a background recording begins.
- Candidate selections and the recording index are refreshed after every clip
  and after the batch finishes, so another batch can be recorded without
  reloading the exported candidates.
- When TF2 closes early, HLAE is allowed to flush before every usable completed
  capture is finalized, indexed, and reflected on the Candidates page.

Airshot behavior:
- Absolute height is ignored; higher ledges do not qualify as airshots.
- The victim must have an observed grounded-to-airborne transition within two
  seconds, at least three airborne samples, and meaningful vertical motion.
- Standard projectile evidence stops two ticks before impact.
- Loose Cannon evidence stops six ticks before the first collision/hurt event,
  preventing the Cannon's own knockback from certifying a double donk or later
  explosion as an airshot.
- Five regression tests cover stationary and moving elevated ledges, genuine
  pre-impact airborne motion, collision-induced Cannon launch, and genuine
  pre-existing airborne Cannon victims.
- Confirmed airshots receive an additional 0-20 style points based on both the
  victim's positive height relative to the attacker and the upward elevation
  angle from attacker to victim; absolute map Z never earns points.
- New searchable tags identify high-relative, steep-skyward, and extreme
  skyward airshots.
- Two geometry regression tests verify that a shared high ledge earns no bonus
  and that steeper, higher airshots score progressively more.

Preserved behavior:
- Recording Settings autosave, including initial default settings persistence.
- Saved FFmpeg, HLAE, TF2, and output-directory paths.
- Startup recording-session recovery hardening.
- Vote, server, plugin, and chat-message suppression during capture.
- Previously recorded candidates can be skipped or explicitly re-recorded.

Validation performed in this environment:
- Source references to camera UI/model/VDM features were removed.
- Rust delimiter and cross-reference checks were run.
- Archive source assertions and ZIP integrity are checked during packaging.

The Rust toolchain is not installed in this transient environment. Run
BUILD_RUST_APP.bat on Windows for the complete Rust/Slint compilation.
